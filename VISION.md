# AI Work OS Vision

## Mission

Build the operating system where humans define goals and AI teams make them happen.

## From Tasks to Missions

AI Work OS moves beyond task management. Users define outcomes; AI systems create execution plans.

## AI Workforce

Agents are specialized digital workers with skills, memory, tools, and responsibilities.

## Trust

Every important AI action should provide evidence, history, and human approval when required.

## Organizational Memory

AI Work OS preserves decisions, context, architecture, and knowledge so organizations improve over time.

## Future

The next generation of software will not only help people manage work. It will help people achieve more.
