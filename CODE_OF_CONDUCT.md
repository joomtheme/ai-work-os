# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

AI Work OS welcomes contributors from all backgrounds. We are committed to a respectful, collaborative and constructive community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Welcome different perspectives
- Provide constructive feedback
- Focus on improving the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Deliberate disruption of the community

Thank you for helping create a positive open source environment. 🚀
